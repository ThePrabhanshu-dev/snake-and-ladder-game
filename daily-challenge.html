How the Daily Challenge works

  1. The board is seeded from today's date, so the dice sequence is identical for every player.
  2. Start the challenge and you play that shared sequence — 2 to 4 players as usual.
  3. Because the rolls are fixed, every outcome is a decision you made, not a roll you lost.
  4. Share the challenge link with a friend so they play your exact board.

It is the fastest way to get better at the game: replay today's board and try to beat your own result, then compare scores with friends on the same sequence.

Why a shared board is better for practice

In a normal game, every decision is tangled up with chance. A 6 might rescue you or ruin you and you can never tell whether your decision was good. In the Daily Challenge the dice are fixed, so every result is a pure read on your play.

  • Did you take the bonus six when you should have banked it?
  • Did you chase a ladder base, or did you drift into the snake at 87?
  • Did you protect a lead, or did you roll greedily for a ladder?

Answer the same three questions on the same board each day and your play improves quickly.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Snake & Ladder Daily Challenge?

It is a daily board where every player receives the exact same dice sequence. The date is used to seed the random number generator, so everyone rolls the same numbers in the same order. That turns a game of pure luck into a test of decision making.

When does the new daily board appear?

A new board starts at midnight local time and stays live for the whole day. The seed is derived from the date, so everyone playing on the same calendar day is on the same board.

How many players can join a daily challenge?

Between 2 and 4, exactly like a normal game. The dice sequence is shared, so each player rolls in turn from the same sequence of numbers.

Can I replay the same daily board?

You can replay the board as often as you like, but the dice sequence never changes, so re-running it produces exactly the same rolls. Practising the same board is how most players improve their openings.

How do I compare my score with friends?

Use the Challenge a friend button inside the game. It copies a link that includes the challenge date, so opening it drops your friend straight onto the same board you played.
