Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Snake & Ladder Online ("the Game") is a free, single-page browser game. It has no account system, no sign-up, no server database and no user profiles. This policy explains what happens to data when you play.

Information we collect

We do not collect names, email addresses or any personally identifiable information from players. The Game stores a single value in your browser's localStorage: the referral code (snl_ref) of the partner site that sent you here, so that referral links can be credited correctly. This stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

Advertising and cookies

This site is funded by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Google publishes third-party party cookie policies at policies.google.com.

Third-party vendors set these cookies directly in your browser when you view ads on this site. The Game itself does not read, write or share advertising cookies.

Depending on which ad network is active, ads on this site may also be served by other advertising partners (for example Adsterra or similar networks). Those partners may use their own cookies or local storage to deliver and measure ads. You can opt out of third-party advertising cookies at aboutads.info.

Third-party links

Pages on this site link to other websites (game portals, social platforms and referral partners). We are not responsible for the content, privacy practices or cookies of those sites. Please review their policies before you share personal information with them.

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data processing and hosting

The static files of this site are served by Google Cloud and Cloudflare. Their servers process standard technical request data (such as IP address and user agent) to deliver the page and to prevent abuse. Our own hosting logs are not retained.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. You can clear the referral code at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site or its ad partners change. The "last updated" date above always reflects the current version.
