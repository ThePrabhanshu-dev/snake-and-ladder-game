How to play
  • Land on a ladder base to climb to its top.
  • Land on a snake head to slide down its tail.
  • Need an exact 100 to win — overshooting ends your turn.
  • Three 6s in a row sends you back to square 1!
  • Press the space bar to roll, or click the board.
