How to play Ludo
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard onto your start square.
  • Land on a single opponent token to send it back to its yard — safe squares (★) cannot be captured.
  • Two opponents on one square block the road: you cannot pass or land there.
  • A six gives you another turn. Three sixes in a row sends your token back to the yard.
  • Bring all four tokens into the centre column to win the game.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Ludo online?

Every player rolls a six-sided die. You need a six to bring a token out of the yard and onto the board. Move your tokens around the 56-square track, capture a single opponent token by landing on it, avoid squares blocked by two opponents, and bring all four of your tokens into the centre column to win.

How many players can play Ludo on this site?

Between two and four. Players take turns on the same device, or you can switch on the computer opponent and play against the machine.

What happens if I roll a six?

A six grants you an extra turn, which is how you bring new tokens out of your yard. Rolling three sixes in a row is a foul: the token goes back to the yard and the turn passes to the next player.

Which squares are safe in Ludo?

The four start squares and the four squares directly in front of each home column are safe. A token on a safe square can never be captured.

Do I need an account to play Ludo here?

No. The whole game runs in your browser with no signup, no download and no server. Your game statistics are stored on your own device only.
