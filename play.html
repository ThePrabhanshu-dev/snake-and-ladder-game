How to play Snake & Ladder

Roll the dice and move your token up the 100-square board. Land on the bottom of a ladder and you climb to the top. Land on the head of a snake and you slide back down to its tail.

  • Roll a 6You roll again immediately — use the bonus turn to chain your way up the board.
  • Exact 100 winsYou need to land on exactly 100. If you overshoot, you stay put and lose the turn.
  • Three 6s in a rowYou get sent back to the start. Careful with your lucky rolls!
  • Watch the snakesSquares 16, 62, 64, 87, 93 and 95 send you sliding backwards.
